Do not commit/distribute Yamaha firmware with this source project.

Supported firmware: original Yamaha DX7 Mk I v1.8 (IG11469).
Special Edition / SER-7 firmware is not supported.
Successful loading of another firmware image does not establish compatibility.
See docs/guides/GUIDE_EN.md or docs/guides/GUIDE_HU.md for firmware identification.

For local testing you may place your own ROM here temporarily, but the plugin itself auto-searches:

macOS:
~/Library/Application Support/VDX7-JUCE/ROM/
~/Library/Application Support/discoDSP/Retromulator/ROM/

Windows:
%USERPROFILE%\Documents\VDX7-JUCE\ROM\
%USERPROFILE%\Documents\discoDSP\Retromulator\ROM\

Supported layouts using v1.8:
- 16384-byte DX7 Mk I v1.8 firmware (e.g. DX7-V1-8.OBJ; verify content, not just the name)
- 49152-byte combined dx7.bin (16 KB v1.8 firmware + 32 KB valid factory voice data)
